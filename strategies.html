What we do

Six strategies. One entrepreneurial thread.

Across private equity, private credit, venture capital, real estate, expansion capital and wealth, every Sagard strategy shares the same conviction: back great operators with patient, flexible capital.

01

Sagard Private Equity Solutions

Investing across both LP-led and GP-led secondary transactions as well as direct control investments, with a sustained focus on the global mid-market. Following our combination with Unigestion, the platform manages one of the industry's most comprehensive private equity solutions offerings.

FocusMid-market, LP & GP-led
AUM$12.5bn+
GeographyNorth America & Europe
02

Private Credit

Flexible, direct lending solutions for growing companies that value speed and certainty of execution. Our evergreen Sagard Private Credit Fund (SPCF), launched in 2024, opened this strategy to Canadian accredited investors for the first time.

StructureEvergreen & closed-end
FlagshipSagard Private Credit Fund
LaunchedSeptember 2024
03

Portage — Venture Capital

Portage backs category-defining fintech and technology founders from seed through growth stages, pairing capital with deep operating and regulatory expertise across financial services.

FocusFintech & technology
StageSeed to growth
GeographyGlobal
04

Sagard Real Estate

A full spectrum of commercial real estate strategies — separate accounts and commingled funds — spanning all major property sectors and points in the capital stack, for institutional and private clients alike.

VehiclesSeparate accounts, commingled funds
SectorsAll major property types
GeographyUnited States
05

Sagard NewGen

Majority and minority growth investments of €10M–€50M (excluding co-investors), with or without leverage, financing the expansion of profitable, Europe-based companies generating €10M–€150M in revenue.

Ticket size€10M – €50M

GeographyEurope

Sagard Wealth
