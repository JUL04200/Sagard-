About Sagard

Built by entrepreneurs. Still run like one.

Since 2002, Sagard has partnered with founders and management teams to build resilient, category-leading businesses — bringing patient capital, deep sector expertise and a genuinely global network to every partnership.

Our story

A family's conviction, grown into a global platform.

Sagard began as a partnership between the Desmarais family, through Power Corporation, and a small group of European industrial families who shared a single belief: that entrepreneurs deserve capital partners who think in decades, not quarters. Under the leadership of Paul Desmarais III since 2016, that founding conviction has scaled into a $46bn multi-strategy platform spanning private equity, private credit, venture capital and real estate — without ever losing its founder-first culture.

Today, more than 540 investment and operating professionals work across 15 offices in North America and Europe, supported by the network, patience and long-term orientation of our founding families.

History

Two decades of patient, entrepreneurial capital.

2002

Sagard is founded as a partnership between the Desmarais family, through Power Corporation of Canada, and a network of European industrial families — with a mandate to back entrepreneurs building the middle market.

2016

Paul Desmarais III takes the helm, sharpening Sagard's focus and setting the platform on a path of deliberate, multi-strategy expansion.

2019 – 2023

Sagard expands beyond private equity into private credit, venture capital (Portage) and real estate, while extending its footprint from Paris and Montreal into New York, Toronto, London and beyond.

2024

Launch of the evergreen Sagard Private Credit Fund (SPCF), opening private credit access to Canadian accredited investors for the first time.

2025

Sagard and Unigestion combine, creating a global leader in middle-market private equity investment solutions and pushing platform AUM toward $46bn.

2026 & beyond

Sagard continues to broaden retail and institutional access to private markets, with new evergreen strategies planned across North America.
