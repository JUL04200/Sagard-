History

Built by combination: launches, acquisitions and partnerships.

2002

Sagard's origins.

2016

Sagard creates the global, multi-strategy platform.

2017

Launch of Sagard Credit Partners.

2019

Launch of Sagard Healthcare Partners.

2020

Sagard acquires Sagard SAS and launches Sagard NewGen.

2021

Launch of Sagard Private Equity Canada and Senior Lending Partners; EverWest acquisition.

2022 – 2024

Partnerships with ADQ, BMO, HalseyPoint and BEX Capital; Performance Equity Management integration; GBL becomes a shareholder.

2025

Sagard and Unigestion announce their combination.

2026

The Unigestion transaction is finalized.
